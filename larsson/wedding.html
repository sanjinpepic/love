Getting to Järvsö

Järvsö lies in the Ljusnan valley in Hälsingland, a little over three hundred kilometres north of Stockholm. The train is the easy way.

By train

Trains run from Stockholm Central to Järvsö station, in the middle of the village, in about three hours — via Uppsala, Gävle and Bollnäs. Many northbound trains also stop at Arlanda C, right at the airport.

By car

About four hours from Stockholm: the E4 north, then road 83 up along the Ljusnan river.

By air

Fly to Stockholm Arlanda and continue by train, or to Sundsvall–Timrå, about two hours away by car.

Where to stay

Järvsö is a small village and late August is a lovely time to be there, so it’s wise to book early.

We’ll share a short list of places to stay nearby with the invitation. If you’d like to make a few days of it, the valley is made for walks, swims and long evenings.

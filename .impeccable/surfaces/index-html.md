# Surface brief: Klimt variant (Save the Date + wedding page)

Scope: `index.html`, `wedding.html` (the main version; the couple chose Klimt). Mode: Persuade/Experience for the Save the Date, Read + one Operate task (RSVP) for the wedding page. The Larsson version stays at `/larsson/`; same copy, same RSVP server. Code-led: the paintings are drawn live in canvas by `assets/js/klimt.js`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Klimt's landscapes, not his gold: a square painting made of thousands of small dabs, hung on a quiet ground and labelled like a museum print. Minimal: one painting, spaced capitals, one italic line, the date. Refuses the gold-and-ornament Klimt cliché, fat buttons, and calendar-link clutter.

OWN-WORLD: cool near-white print paper (#f6f7f3), grey-green slate lettering (#44524f), Klimt's dab palette (yellow-greens, greens, violet-blue shadow) only inside the paintings; violet (#4f569c) for times, hover and the chosen radio. Tenor Sans spaced capitals for names, headings and links; Alegreya italic for the subtitle and asides.

STORY: Järvsöklack seen across the Ljusnan (after Litzlberg on the Attersee) → names, "are getting married", place, date → "Add to calendar" / "The wedding page" as quiet text links.

FIRST VIEWPORT: desktop: the square painting at left (min(76svh, 54vw)), the label centred in the right column. Phone: the square on top, the label below, like the print.

FORM: pinned by the couple (Klimt landscapes: Litzlberg, Pear Tree); seed:ebf211c301f16672 (rolled for the record; the direction is pinned). Signature interaction: the painting lays itself down dab by dab over ~2.6 s; the river reflection keeps drifting; layers shift slightly with the pointer. Reduced motion: painted at once, still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
